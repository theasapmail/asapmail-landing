Frequently asked questions

Will my email look right on a phone?
Yes. The email is built at 600 px and restacks for mobile screens: columns collapse, buttons stay tappable, type does not shrink. The linter checks the responsive rules as well.
Can I check my design before leaving my email?
You can. Send a mockup screenshot or your site URL in reply to the invitation — we will tell you what can be built as is and what has to be simplified for Outlook, and why. You can also run your existing HTML through our linter right now.
How many revisions are included?
Unlimited during the beta. Edits are made in words (“move the button up”, “drop the banner”): only the affected section is rebuilt, not the whole email, and the linter checks it again.
Will it really not fall apart in Outlook and dark mode?
Markup follows the constraints of email clients, and the linter checks every section against the same rules and will not release an email with errors. Backgrounds are pinned with fills so dark mode does not recolour your brand. Warnings stay in the report — we list them rather than hide them.
Which sending tools does it work with?
Any ESP that accepts your own HTML: Mailchimp, Customer.io, Resend, SendGrid, Unisender and others. Unsubscribe, sender and main-button addresses are placeholders such as {{UNSUBSCRIBE_URL}} — your ESP fills in its own.
